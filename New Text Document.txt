# Ph.D. Position: AI and Quantum Computing for Single-Cell Biology

**Fully funded Ph.D. position**
**Cai Laboratory – Texas A&M University**

The **Cai Laboratory** at **Texas A&M University** is recruiting an exceptional Ph.D. student to help build the next generation of computational methods for **single-cell biology**. We are seeking a student who is excited not only to apply existing tools, but to **invent fundamentally new algorithms** at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, machine learning, network science, and quantum computing**.

This position is ideal for candidates who want to pursue high-impact, high-risk research that could redefine how cellular systems are modeled and analyzed.

---

## Vision

Modern single-cell technologies can profile millions of cells, yet we still lack principled computational frameworks for understanding:

* How stable cellular states emerge from gene regulatory networks
* How cells transition between states during development and disease
* How multi-omic information can be integrated into predictive models
* How quantum-inspired and quantum-native algorithms might overcome current computational bottlenecks

Our laboratory is developing new mathematical and computational foundations for these problems, including **AI-driven representation learning, graph neural networks, dynamical systems, causal network inference, and quantum machine learning approaches for single-cell data**.

---

## Research Areas

The successful student may contribute to one or more of the following frontier projects:

* Foundation models for single-cell and spatial omics
* Generative models of cellular identity and cell-state dynamics
* Gene regulatory network inference and attractor-state modeling
* Causal discovery from perturbation and multi-omic data
* Quantum kernels and quantum machine learning for high-dimensional biological data
* QUBO/Ising formulations of biological inference problems
* Scalable algorithms for atlases containing millions of cells
* Open-source software platforms for the biomedical research community

Applications span **cancer, cardiovascular disease, stroke, neuroscience, aging, and precision medicine**.

---

## What Makes This Position Unique

This is not a routine bioinformatics position. The student will have the opportunity to:

* Help define a new research direction in **quantum computational biology**
* Publish methodological work in leading journals in computational biology, machine learning, and interdisciplinary science
* Collaborate with researchers in biology, medicine, computer science, statistics, physics, and engineering
* Gain experience in both theoretical algorithm development and large-scale biomedical data analysis
* Present research at major international conferences in genomics, AI, and quantum information science

Strong students will be encouraged to develop independent research programs early in their Ph.D. training.
